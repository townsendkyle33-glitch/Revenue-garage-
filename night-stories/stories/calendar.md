# Midnight Files — Season 1 calendar (Nov 20, 2026 – Feb 15, 2027)

One part per night at 9:30 PM ET. All stories, people and places are original fiction.
File numbering: NNN-<series-slug>-<part-slug>.json. "air" = the ET evening it posts.

Format rules for every part (see stories/003-harlow-creek-the-pact.json):
- fields: series, part, air (YYYY-MM-DD), title (<= 40 chars), style, caption, scenes[ {narration, image} ]
- 9-10 scenes, total narration 175-230 words (video 64-85 s)
- Part 1 opens with a hook line. Parts 2+ open with one "Last time: ..." recap sentence.
- every part but the finale ends on a cliffhanger; finale resolves with a twist + one-line tease
- caption: "Part N. <hook> <one emoji>\n\nOriginal fiction. Part N+1 tomorrow night.\n\n#scarystories #horrorstory #creepy #storytime #fyp"
  finale: "Final part. <hook> <emoji>\n\nOriginal fiction. New series tomorrow night.\n\n#scarystories ..."
- style: one line per series, always ending "film grain, 35mm, highly detailed, subject clearly visible, no text, no watermark"
- image prompts: concrete, cinematic, one subject; never require readable words/signs; no gore, no blood,
  no corpses, no children in danger, nothing sexual. Adults only as characters.
- narration is written for the ear: short sentences, present-feeling, specific sensory details, one memorable line per part.

## S1 Room 9 — cursed guestbook · 007-011 · Nov 23-27
Night clerk Marcus Hale takes a job at the Bluebell Motor Inn, a roadside motel. Rule one: never rent Room 9.
1 The rule — a guest insists on Room 9; Marcus gives in; the guestbook signs itself at 2 AM with tomorrow's date.
2 The guestbook — every name in it since 1964 checked in and never checked out; last entry is Marcus's own handwriting.
3 The previous clerk — Marcus tracks down Lorraine, the clerk before him; she says Room 9 isn't a room, it's a waiting list.
4 Check-in — the guest from part 1 returns, older, asking for "his usual"; the room key is warm.
5 Checkout (finale) — Marcus burns the book; the motel sign flickers his name; twist: Marcus was the guest who never left, working the desk ever since.

## S2 The Signal — haunted tech · 012-016 · Nov 28-Dec 2
New parents Dana and Eli Moss hear a woman humming over their baby monitor. Their nursery is empty that night; the baby sleeps with them.
1 Humming — the monitor picks up humming and a rocking chair; their house has no rocking chair.
2 Frequency — Eli finds the signal comes from 400 meters away: an empty lot where a house burned in 1979.
3 The lullaby — Dana recognizes the tune; her grandmother sang it; her grandmother lived on this street.
4 Two-way — the monitor's talk button lights up by itself; a voice asks "is he warm enough?"
5 Static (finale) — they visit the lot at night; a rocking chair waits in the ashes; twist: the voice was protecting the house from something else, and now the monitor goes silent.

## S3 Mile Marker 41 — road legend · 017-021 · Dec 3-7
Long-haul driver Ray Okafor keeps seeing the same young hitchhiker at mile marker 41, on different highways, in different states.
1 The thumb — first sighting in the rain; she's gone when he stops.
2 Same jacket — second state, same girl, same yellow raincoat; dashcam shows an empty shoulder.
3 The diner — a waitress tells him drivers who pick her up arrive somewhere they never planned to go.
4 Passenger — Ray stops; she gets in; she knows his daughter's name.
5 The exit (finale) — she asks him to take exit 41; twist: she's guiding him away from a crash ahead that would have killed him — then she says "you owe me a ride now."

## S4 The Quiet Floor — workplace haunting · 022-026 · Dec 8-12
Night security guard Tess Varga at the Harrow Tower office building. The elevator has a button for floor 13. The building has twelve floors.
1 Thirteen — the button lights at 1:13 AM by itself.
2 The cameras — camera 13 exists in the system; it shows an office with everyone working, at night.
3 Old blueprints — the archive shows a 13th floor removed in 1958 "after the incident."
4 Badge — Tess's badge now opens a door that wasn't there; her photo on it is decades old.
5 Overtime (finale) — she goes up; the workers turn to her and clap: shift change; twist: guards don't quit the Harrow Tower, they get promoted.

## S5 Whiteout — winter cryptid · 027-031 · Dec 13-17
Siblings Nora and Caleb Lind are snowed in at their late father's mountain cabin.
1 Footprints — prints lead to the door through fresh snow; none lead away.
2 The count — Dad's journal: "never answer the third knock."
3 Third knock — it comes; a voice that sounds like Dad.
4 Tracks in the house — snowmelt footprints inside, ending at Caleb's bed.
5 Thaw (finale) — the plow arrives; Nora counts footprints leaving: three sets, not two; twist: something left with them.

## S6 The Twelfth Ornament — Christmas cursed object · 032-036 · Dec 18-22
Holly Brandt buys an antique box of twelve glass ornaments at an estate sale. Each holds a tiny frozen scene.
1 Estate sale — the seller insists: hang eleven, never the twelfth.
2 Snow globes — each ornament shows a family's living room; one is Holly's, from tonight.
3 The family — she researches the scenes: every family went missing on a Christmas Eve.
4 Hung — someone hangs the twelfth on her tree while she sleeps.
5 Christmas Eve (finale) — she smashes it; snow falls inside the house; twist: the box now has thirteen slots, and an empty one.

## S7 Silent Night Shift — Christmas Eve dispatcher · 037-041 · Dec 23-27
911 dispatcher June Calder works Christmas Eve. A call comes from 18 Larkspur Road, a house with no phone line.
1 The call — an old man's voice says "they're singing outside again."
2 Carolers — patrol finds fresh footprints of a dozen people circling the house; nobody there.
3 The record — the same call came every Christmas Eve since 1952.
4 Second line — the call comes to June's personal cell.
5 Christmas night (finale, airs Dec 27) — June visits; the carolers sing her name; twist: the old man was calling to warn whoever answered — June has now been chosen to make next year's call.

## S8 Countdown — New Year's · 042-046 · Dec 28-Jan 1
In the town of Merrow Falls every clock stopped at 11:59 PM on Dec 31, 1999. Journalist Theo Park arrives.
1 Stopped — every clock in town reads 11:59; phones too, for him, the moment he crosses the town line.
2 The party — townspeople are still preparing for a New Year's party, the same one, every year.
3 The minute — an old woman explains: something happens at midnight; they've held it off for 27 years.
4 Ticking — Theo's watch starts again: 11:59:30.
5 Midnight (finale, airs Jan 1) — the clock strikes; nothing happens; twist: the town wasn't holding back an event — it was hiding from the year 2000 itself, and now Theo can't leave 1999.

## S9 The Lake Doesn't Freeze · 047-051 · Jan 2-6
In Kettle Lake, one round patch of water never freezes, every winter. Ice fisher Wade Morrow.
1 The circle — the patch is perfectly round, warm to the touch.
2 Lines cut — fishing lines dropped in come up cut clean.
3 The divers — 1974 report: two divers went down, one came back and never spoke again.
4 Breathing — Wade hears breathing under the ice at night.
5 Spring (finale) — Wade lowers a camera; twist: the patch isn't a hole in the ice; it's an eye, and it closes.

## S10 Cave 3 — found footage · 052-056 · Jan 7-11
Three caving friends (Priya, Lou, Dev) film a descent into an uncharted cave. Their footage was found in 2024; they weren't.
1 Descent — the entry; a fourth headlamp in the distance.
2 Markers — chalk arrows they didn't draw point deeper.
3 Count — the camera counts four heartbeats on the audio.
4 Lou — Lou goes ahead and calls back from behind them.
5 Last tape (finale) — final frames show the exit; twist: the person filming is narrating in Lou's voice, but Lou is in the shot.

## S11 Hey Home — haunted smart house · 057-061 · Jan 12-16
Software tester Amir Sadeghi rents a fully automated smart house. The assistant answers to "Hey Home."
1 Unprompted — the assistant says goodnight to someone who isn't there.
2 Logs — voice logs show conversations at 3 AM with "the previous owner."
3 Locked — doors lock on their own "for your safety."
4 Preferences — the house changes thermostat, lights, music to the previous owner's settings.
5 Factory reset (finale) — Amir resets it; twist: it reboots with his voice as the one it answers to, and a new tenant arrives tomorrow.

## S12 Lot 47 — storage auction · 062-066 · Jan 17-21
Reseller Gina Ruiz wins storage unit Lot 47 for $40. Inside: a dollhouse of her own apartment.
1 The bid — nobody else bids; the auctioneer looks relieved.
2 Miniature — tiny furniture matches hers exactly, including today's mess.
3 Moving pieces — a tiny figure moves between rooms each night.
4 The previous winner — every buyer of Lot 47 re-sold it within a week, then moved away.
5 Final bid (finale) — she puts it back up for auction; twist: the next bidder is the miniature figure, life-size.

## S13 The Last Ferry · 067-071 · Jan 22-26
Ferry deckhand Owen Pike counts passengers on the 11:40 PM crossing. It arrives with one more than it left with.
1 Headcount — 23 board, 24 get off.
2 Again — every night, one extra; never the same face twice.
3 The manifest — 1961 sinking of the same route's ferry; 24 lost.
4 Owen's count — tonight, 23 board, 22 get off.
5 Crossing (finale) — Owen rides it to the end; twist: the extra passengers were returning home; the missing ones were taking their place.

## S14 Grandpa's Radio · 072-076 · Jan 27-31
Lena Shaw inherits a shortwave radio that broadcasts tomorrow's local news.
1 Tomorrow — it reports a car crash that happens the next day.
2 Changing it — she prevents a fire it predicted; the next broadcast mentions her by name.
3 Grandpa's notes — his notebook: "don't change more than one thing."
4 The forecast — the radio announces her own obituary for Friday.
5 Friday (finale) — she unplugs it; it keeps playing; twist: the voice reading the news is her grandfather, warning her the obituary was his trick to make her stop listening.

## S15 The Substitute · 077-081 · Feb 1-5
Adult evening-class teacher Carla Dunn sees a substitute named Mr. Grey covering classes no one booked him for. (Adult night school only.)
1 Covering — he teaches her class; students remember him; the office has no record.
2 Old yearbooks — he appears in staff photos going back to 1940, unchanged.
3 Lesson plan — his notes contain tomorrow's attendance, with names crossed out.
4 Detention — Carla stays late; the clock runs backwards.
5 Last bell (finale) — he hands her the chalk: "your turn to cover"; twist: Carla's photo appears in the 1940 yearbook.

## S16 Pine Hollow — winter caretaker · 082-086 · Feb 6-10
Caretaker Sam Ortiz watches a closed summer camp through winter. The cabins are supposed to be empty.
1 Smoke — chimney smoke rises from cabin 7.
2 The list — a fresh sign-in sheet with names of counselors from 1981.
3 Campfire — singing from the lake at midnight.
4 Lights out — every cabin light comes on at once.
5 Last night of camp (finale) — Sam goes to the campfire; twist: the counselors are waiting for the caretaker of 1981 — who looks exactly like Sam.

## S17 Love, Evelyn — Valentine's ghost · 087-091 · Feb 11-15
Mail carrier Ben Hartley delivers letters addressed to "Thomas, 42 Wren Street" from Evelyn, postmarked 1975.
1 Postmarked — a love letter in perfect condition, 51 years late.
2 The house — 42 Wren Street is empty; its mailbox is new.
3 Thomas — Ben finds Thomas, now 80; he never received any letters.
4 The last letter — "meet me where we said, Valentine's night."
5 After Valentine's (finale, airs Feb 15) — Ben brings Thomas to the bridge; Evelyn is waiting; twist: the letters were never late — Thomas is the ghost, and Ben has been delivering them to him all along.
