# Series: Harlow Creek (original fiction)

Premise: in the small town of Harlow Creek nobody locks their doors. In 1987 the Mercer family
locked theirs and vanished at 3:12 AM. Every part: one-line recap opener, 170-230 words,
ends on a cliffhanger (except the finale). On-screen tag "Harlow Creek · Part N".

Characters (all fictional): Jenna Cole (podcaster, moves to town to investigate),
Tom & Ruth Mercer (vanished 1987), Mrs. Edith Pruitt (oldest resident), Sheriff Dale Boone.
"The Guest" = the thing that walks through Harlow Creek's open doors at 3:12 AM.

1. The town that never locks its doors — the Mercers lock up, vanish at 3:12. (posted Nov 17)
2. The tape — Jenna finds Tom's camcorder tape in the sheriff's evidence room. The last
   minute was never reported: Tom whispering "it doesn't need to knock. It already lives here."
   Cliff: at 3:12 her own front door, which she locked, clicks open.
3. The pact — Mrs. Pruitt tells her about the 1911 agreement: every house leaves its door
   open so the Guest can pass through. It only takes from houses that shut it out.
   Cliff: Mrs. Pruitt's porch light goes out, one bulb at a time, toward Jenna.
4. Backwards — Jenna plays the tape in reverse and hears Ruth Mercer counting down from 312.
   Cliff: the count matches the number of days since the Mercers vanished... minus one.
5. Alder Lane — Jenna enters the abandoned Mercer house at night. Every door is open except one.
   Cliff: knocking from the inside.
6. Finale: The Guest — the door opens; the Mercers are there, unchanged since 1987, keeping the
   doors of the town open for the Guest. Twist ending: the town was never protecting itself; it was
   feeding it, and Jenna's locked door was the invitation. Last line hook for the next series.
After the finale: start a new original series (different sub-genre), 4-6 parts, same rules.
