# Wasted Daily — Playbook

**Handle:** @WastedDailyclip (X, max 15 chars) · use `wasteddailyclip` everywhere else
**Platforms:** X, Instagram (Professional/Creator), TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Threads
**Scheduler:** Buffer · **Clipping:** OpusClip (pending) · **Editor:** CapCut
**Time zone:** US Eastern (ET). Launch night timed to NZ/AU unlock.
**Approval:** auto-post for routine clips. Owner approval only for: rumors, paid/sponsored, plan upgrades or purchases, name/bio changes.

## Bio (all platforms)
GTA funny moments, cars & RP clips from the best streamers. Credited, daily.
Fan page. Not affiliated with Rockstar Games.

## Niche & pillars
| Pillar | What | Share of posts |
|---|---|---|
| Funny moments | Streamer fails, NPC chaos, ragdolls, Wasted moments | ~40% |
| RP moments | Roleplay scenes from RP servers: cops, heists, court, chaos | ~30% |
| Cars | Customization, chases, rare finds, stunts | ~20% |
| News / launch | Official Rockstar news only, countdown, "OUT NOW" | ~10% (more on news days) |

## Volume
4–6 posts/day per platform. Quality beats volume: skip a slot rather than post something weak.

## Daily slots (ET)
1. 9:00 — news recap or funny clip
2. 11:30 — RP moment
3. 14:00 — car clip
4. 17:00 — funny clip
5. 19:30 — RP moment
6. 22:00 — best clip of the day / countdown

## Voice
Hype but clean. Short punchy hook, sentence case, max one emoji (🌴 ⏳ 👀 🎥). Never clickbait.

## Every clip
- Hook in the first second (on-screen text)
- 7–30s trim, cut dead air, end on the payoff
- Burned-in captions, large, centered, high contrast
- Add our own value: label, text commentary, or compilation framing
- Credit line: `🎥 @creator` (on-platform handle of the person who made the clip)
- No spoilers in hook/thumbnail during launch week
- Specs: 9:16, 1080x1920, MP4 H.264 + AAC

## Hard rules (even on auto-post)
- Public clips only. Credit every creator. Take down same day if asked, log it in takedowns.md, stop using that creator.
- No leaks, datamines, piracy, "play early" tricks, exploits, fake news, AI-faked gameplay.
- Never imply we're Rockstar. No fake giveaways.
- Facts only from official sources (facts.md).
- Never post a number we can't reproduce.
