# Revenue Garage

Command center for the Revenue Garage businesses. One goal: **$100,000 a month in real, collected cash.**

## What's here

`index.html` is the whole app. It's one page with no build step.

- **Goal bar**: verified cash this month vs. the monthly goal, sales closed, average per sale, and how much is needed per day for the rest of the month.
- **The bays**: one card per business, each with a status (Live / Pilot / Off) and one next action.
  - The Pipeline: water filtration sales & lead gen
  - The Craftsman: Etsy
  - The Trendsetter: TikTok / social commerce
  - The Quant: trading research, **paper only**. It never counts toward real cash.
- **The Pipeline funnel**: leads → contacted → appointments set → sat → sales → cash collected, with conversion rates.
- **Road to the goal**: how many sales, appointments and leads the goal takes, worked out from your own conversion rates once you've logged enough. Until then it uses labeled assumptions.
- **Activity log**: every entry, by month. Tap × twice to delete.

## Rules the numbers follow

- Only **cash collected** counts as verified cash. Sales, appointments and leads are counted, but they aren't money.
- Paper trading is never added to real cash.
- No number is invented. Everything on the page comes from something you logged.

## Where data is saved

- **Live page on claude.ai**: entries are saved to your account and sync across devices. Only you (and anyone you make an Editor) can see or change them.
- **Opened as a plain file / other hosting**: entries are saved in that browser only.
